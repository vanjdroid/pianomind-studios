🔒 PianoMind Studios — Privacy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version

PianoMind Studios is a free piano practice app. There are no ads. We do not sell your data. We do not track you around the internet.

You can play as a guest. Then nothing leaves your device.

What we save if you make an account

Your email address, so you can sign in. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email and name.

The name you type in the app.

Your practice progress: scores, XP, levels, streaks, trophies, and settings like your theme and piano sound.

Your profile picture, if you upload one. It is made small before saving. Only you see it.

Applause (only if you use it): your 6-letter friend code, the nicknames you give friends, and the claps you get. A clap is only an icon. No names or messages are ever sent.

Where it is kept

On your device, so the app works offline.

In Google Firebase, a service run by Google that stores accounts and data for apps. Safety rules let each person read and change only their own data.

Other services the app talks to

Google Fonts (the letters you see) and Firebase code are loaded from Google. Like any website, Google can see your device's internet address when this happens.

GitHub Pages hosts the app's files.

Who can see your data

Only you, when signed in. The app's owner can see the list of accounts in Firebase to keep the app running. We never share it with anyone else.

How to delete everything

In the app: Me → Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your account, your progress, your friend code and your claps.

Or open this link and sign in: vanjdroid.github.io/pianomind-studios/piano_mind.html#delete

Guests: Settings → Delete everything on this device.

We keep your data until you delete it.

Kids

This app is made for children. Kids, please ask a parent or carer before you make an account. Guest mode needs no account at all.
